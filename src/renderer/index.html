BEFORE CAMERA ACCESS

Camera & data consent

Let the participant review this themselves. Do not agree on their behalf. The camera stays off unless they agree, and they can stop it or withdraw at any time.

◉

Camera access

  • Agreeing starts SmartSpectra with a live camera stream. No microphone.
  • Frames are processed on this computer. Video is never recorded or sent to the session backend.
  • Stop the measurement or withdraw consent at any time. Your OS may also ask for camera permission.
⌁

What is measured

  • Breathing rate only, with confidence and stability when available. The first reading needs a full measurement window.
  • Pulse, HRV, facial expressions and sentiment are not requested.
  • These signals are not evidence of honesty and must not be used for diagnosis, treatment or medical decisions.
▤

Storage, sharing & deletion

  • Saved: question prompts and notes only, in ~/.signal-session/sessions.sqlite3 on this computer.
  • Not saved: camera frames, breathing values and selected media files.
  • Expires 30 days after first save (edits don’t extend it). Expired data is removed the next time the app runs.
  • “Delete saved copy” removes it now. Withdrawing consent stops SmartSpectra and deletes it.
  • The local backend only accepts connections from this computer and shares nothing. Its log shows request paths, not contents.
◇

SDK telemetry & AI

  • SmartSpectra SDK telemetry is turned off.
  • When a measurement starts, the SDK sends one authentication request to Presage using the API key from your local .env file.
  • No AI service is used.

Only the participant may agree. Never monitor anyone without their knowledge and consent. “Not now” closes this without starting the camera.
